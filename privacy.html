Privacy Policy

PunitExplores

Effective September 21, 2026

PunitExplores is a personal application built and operated by Punit Sharda to manage his own YouTube channel, youtube.com/@PunitExplores. This policy explains what data the app accesses through Google APIs, how that data is used, and how you can revoke access.

What this app does

PunitExplores connects to the operator's own YouTube channel to help manage video uploads, scheduling, playlists, comments, and to view channel performance data (views, watch time, subscriber trends). It is a single-user tool: it is not a public service, and it does not act on behalf of any YouTube channel other than the operator's own.

Data we access

When the operator authorizes this app via Google's OAuth consent screen, it may access the following data through the YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API:

  • Basic channel information (channel name, ID, statistics)
  • Video and playlist metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, visibility settings)
  • Video content uploaded through the app
  • Comments and captions on the operator's own videos
  • Channel analytics reports (views, watch time, subscriber changes, traffic sources)

How we use this data

Data retrieved through Google APIs is used exclusively to operate the features described above for the channel operator. It is used only within this app, on the operator's own devices, to display information back to the operator or to make changes the operator explicitly requests (such as publishing a video or updating a playlist).

PunitExplores's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share data obtained from Google APIs with any third party.
  • We do not use this data for advertising or ad-related purposes.
  • We do not use this data to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.
  • We do not allow humans to read this data except the operator, and only for debugging at the operator's own request.

Data storage and retention

OAuth tokens and any cached API responses are stored locally on the operator's own devices, not on a shared or third-party server. Data is retained only as long as needed to provide the app's features and is deleted when no longer needed or when access is revoked.

Revoking access

You (the operator) can revoke this app's access to your Google Account at any time from Google Account, Security, Third-party access. Revoking access immediately stops any further data retrieval.

Children's privacy

This app is an internal tool for a single adult operator and is not directed at, marketed to, or knowingly used by children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL with a revised effective date.
